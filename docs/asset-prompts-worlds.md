# 新世界素材生成记录

生成方式：内置 Image Gen。沿用女儿原画的粗彩笔、鲜艳涂色与纸张留白，新增主题不是原画照片裁切。

## 背景（1536 × 1024）

共同要求：Six-year-old child's felt-tip marker drawing on warm off-white paper; thick uneven dark outlines, naive asymmetric shapes, vivid scribble fills with white gaps. Wide 3:2 distant backdrop, light open center, no player, no foreground platforms, no continuous solid ground line. No text, UI, border or polished vector appearance.

### level-beach.png

A turquoise seaside with distant softly scribbled ocean waves, a red-and-white lighthouse, little sailboat, big striped beach umbrella, a smiling small crab, seashells and a sandcastle. Sky pale cream-blue. Colors aqua, coral, sunshine yellow.

### level-meadow.png

A bright grassy countryside with gently rolling green hills only far in the distance, a little windmill, giant daisies, flying butterflies, a smiling little rabbit and a friendly cow. Wide cream sky and a warm yellow sun. Greens, lemon yellow, raspberry pink.

### level-snow.png

A gentle snowy wonderland: distant pale blue snowy mountain peaks, snowy fir trees, a scarf-wearing snowman, a friendly little penguin, a small igloo, sparse hand-scribbled snowflakes in the cream-white sky. Ice blue, lavender, soft pink, white snow.

### level-forest.png

A friendly enchanted forest with tall wobbly trunks, large leafy green and turquoise canopies framing the top and sides, pink and red spotted mushrooms, a cozy hollow treehouse, a little owl and friendly squirrel, firefly dots. Light cream clearing with no dark or scary areas.

### level-cartoon.png

A playful preschool cartoon fantasy mixing a friendly rounded turquoise robot, small smiling purple dinosaur, a red toy rocket, floating colorful balloons, candy-striped tiny houses and lollipop trees, small pink stars, rainbow arches. All original characters, no logos, no text. Bright cream sky.

## 独立透明宝物

共同要求：One isolated collectible, square, centered, 75% motif, actual transparent background. Six-year-old felt-tip marker style, thick uneven strokes, vibrant scribble fills, no text or additional objects.

- collectible-shell.png: one scallop seashell with broad coral pink and lavender marker ridges and a dark wobbly outline, simple fan silhouette.
- collectible-snowflake.png: one bold ice-blue six-armed snowflake with thick blue-purple outline, short chunky branches and a filled cyan center, readable at tiny game size.
- collectible-mushroom.png: one cute red mushroom with white polka dots, short cream stalk and wobbly dark brown outline, no face.
- collectible-balloon.png: one oval hot-pink and yellow party balloon with a thick wobbly dark outline and short curved purple string.

## paper-texture.png

Square seamless tile, very light neutral gray and white felt-tip marker scribble strokes on white drawing paper; fine grain, irregular overlapping horizontal strokes and small white gaps, uniform density, mostly white. No objects, border, text, shadows or perspective. Tinted in Three.js for sand, snow and candy platforms.
