# 手绘界面素材

使用内置 Image Gen 生成四个空白界面素材。实际文字、图标和点击区域仍由网页渲染，九切片保留彩笔边框。

## panel

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE reusable blank hand-drawn paper panel asset for a six-year-old girl's colorful felt-tip garden game. Square 1024x1024. Warm ivory drawing paper interior with extremely subtle natural grain, nearly white center for legible dark Chinese UI text added later. Thin uneven double-traced warm charcoal/olive pencil outline following a gently irregular rounded rectangle at 12 px from image edges. Corners radius about 35 px. Slightly imperfect hand-cut paper perimeter, tiny warm shadow directly under edge. Drawing occupies 98% canvas. Completely EMPTY center. No words, no symbols, no doodles, no stickers, no objects, no mockup surroundings. It must work as a nine-slice panel texture; center and straight edges can extend without distortion. Actual transparent background only outside paper silhouette. Cheerful handmade children's sketchbook style, not vector, not glossy.

## green

Use case: ui-mockup. ONE blank wide green felt-tip marker button for a child's hand-drawn game. Landscape 1536x512, 3:1 ratio. Rounded rectangular handmade paper label filling 98% canvas, thin wobbly dark forest-green double-traced outline 12px inside edges, 45px corners. Fill with medium light fresh apple-green colored-pencil/felt-tip scribble strokes, visibly uneven overlapping horizontal strokes and tiny paper gaps, but low enough contrast for dark ink text added later. NO text, no icons, no words, no objects, no separate stickers or scene. Soft paper grain, flat front view. Consistent edges suitable for nine-slice scaling, empty center. Transparent background outside silhouette.

## neutral

Use case: ui-mockup. ONE blank wide cream felt-tip marker button for a child's hand-drawn game. Landscape 1536x512, 3:1 ratio. Rounded rectangular handmade paper label filling 98% canvas, thin wobbly olive-gray double-traced pencil outline 12px inside edges, 45px corners. Warm off-white drawing paper interior with very faint pale yellow pencil shading and paper grain, nearly white center for readable dark Chinese text added later. NO text, no icons, no words, no objects, no separate stickers or scene. Flat front view. Consistent edges suitable for nine-slice scaling. Transparent background outside silhouette.

Fill with light candy-pink colored-pencil/felt-tip scribble strokes, uneven overlapping horizontal marks with white paper gaps, low contrast for dark plum Chinese text added later. NO text, no icons, no words, no objects, no separate stickers or scene. Paper grain, flat front view. Consistent edges suitable for nine-slice scaling. Transparent background outside silhouette.

## 字体

使用 [霞鹜文楷官方项目](https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai) 的 LXGWWenKai-Regular.ttf，以 fontTools 无损转换为 WOFF2。本地文件 public/fonts/LXGWWenKai-Regular.woff2；SIL OFL 1.1 全文随包放在 public/fonts/OFL.txt。无远程字体请求。
